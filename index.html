About DSB Energy

DSB Energy is an independent local energy assessment business providing professional EPC and retrofit assessment services.

I am qualified as both a Domestic Energy Assessor and Level 4 Retrofit Assessor and work with a practical, customer-focused approach. My aim is to make the assessment process straightforward while giving clients clear, reliable service.

My retrofit work focuses on improving energy efficiency, comfort and the long-term performance of homes.
